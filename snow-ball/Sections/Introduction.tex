Our study designed and implemented two prototypes of snow globes, each equipped with functions such as sending voice messages, playing received messages by twisting the snow globe, sensing ambient light and displaying the light conditions of the other device's environment in real-time, showing the weather of the other device's location, and creating a snowstorm in the other device by shaking the globe. In this project, my role was designer. To improve the readability of the content, this document is organized around several key milestones rather than weekly reports. Nevertheless, the narrative still progresses based on a clear timeline.
